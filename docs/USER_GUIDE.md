# PhotoShuffle Pro — User & Player Guide 🎮

Welcome to **PhotoShuffle Pro**! This user guide covers everything you need to know about playing, customizing, and mastering the game.

---

## 🕹️ Game Modes

### 1. Change & Swap Pieces Mode (Default & Recommended)
- **Objective**: Freely change and swap any two pieces until the entire photo is restored.
- **Controls**:
  - **Click to Swap**: Click the first piece (it highlights with a glowing purple ring and `SELECTED` badge), then click any other piece to exchange their positions! Click the same piece again to deselect.
  - **Drag and Drop**: Click and drag any piece directly onto another piece. The target tile lights up with an emerald `SWAP HERE` badge. Release to swap instantly!

### 2. Classic Sliding (Swipe Mode)
- **Objective**: Slide tiles one-by-one into the empty space until the entire photo is assembled.
- **Controls**:
  - **Mouse / Touch**: Click or tap any tile next to the empty space to slide it in.
  - **Direct Swap & Drag**: You can also drag pieces to swap them in this mode!
  - **Keyboard Arrows / WASD**:
    - <kbd>↑</kbd> or <kbd>W</kbd>: Move the tile *below* the empty space UP.
    - <kbd>↓</kbd> or <kbd>S</kbd>: Move the tile *above* the empty space DOWN.
    - <kbd>←</kbd> or <kbd>A</kbd>: Move the tile to the *right* of the empty space LEFT.
    - <kbd>→</kbd> or <kbd>D</kbd>: Move the tile to the *left* of the empty space RIGHT.

## 🛠️ Helpful Features

### 👁️ Peek Mode
Need to see how the final image should look?
- Click or hold down the **Peek (P)** button, or tap <kbd>P</kbd> on your keyboard.
- A crystal-clear reference photo will fade in over the puzzle board so you can plan your next moves.

### 🔢 Numbers Overlay
- Toggle the **Numbers** button to display small index badges (1 through $N^2$) on each tile.
- Numbers follow standard book reading order (left-to-right, top-to-bottom).

### 💡 Hint Assistant
- Feeling stuck? Click the **Hint** button.
- The next suggested tile will highlight with an amber pulsating border to guide your next move.

### 🖼️ Custom Photos & Image Uploads
You are not limited to the preset gallery!
- **Upload Local File**: Click the **Upload File** button to select any image from your computer or phone.
- **Image via Web URL**: Click **Image URL**, paste a link to any online image (e.g., Unsplash, Imgur, or personal photo), and click **Apply**.

## 🏆 Strategy Tips for Solving

1. **Solve Row by Row**: In sliding puzzles, start by positioning the top row completely (from left to right), then move down to the second row.
2. **Look for the Green Badges**: Tiles currently sitting in their correct home positions display a small green checkmark icon in their top-right corner.
3. **Corner Tile Technique**: When solving the last two tiles of a row or column, pair them up together before slotting them into the corner!
